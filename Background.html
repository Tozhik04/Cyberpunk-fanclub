NIGHT CITY

THE CITY OF DREAMS

Night City is a fictional futuristic metropolis located on the west coast of the United States, in the state of California. It is the main setting of the video game Cyberpunk 2077, developed by CD Projekt Red.


The city is known for its advanced technology, enormous skyscrapers, neon advertisements and crowded streets. It is a place where humans and technology are deeply connected, with cybernetic implants allowing people to enhance their physical and mental abilities.

History of Night City

    1994:Richard Night establishes the plans for Coronado City, which later becomes Night City. 1998: Richard Night is murdered, leading to political instability.
    2020:Corporate conflicts and gang activities become increasingly influential.
    2023:A major corporate conflict results in a nuclear explosion in the city centre.
    2077:The events of Cyberpunk 2077 take place in a technologically advanced but socially divided Night City.

Important technologies in Night City

Cyberware refers to technological devices implanted into the human body. These implants can improve physical abilities, enhance vision and provide additional functions.


Artificial Intelligence

AI is widely used in Night City for security systems, automated services, transportation and digital communication. Advanced AI systems can perform complex tasks and process large amounts of information.
