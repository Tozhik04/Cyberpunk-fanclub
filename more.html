You May Also Like

Our library of games, anime and tabletop games connected to Cyberpunk. Click a title to read more about it and how it connects to our club.

Video Games

Cyberpunk 2077 2020 · CD PROJEKT RED

An open-world role-playing game. You play as V, a mercenary in Night City, who shares their mind with the rebel rockerboy Johnny Silverhand, played by Keanu Reeves.

Connection to our club: The game that brought most of our members to Night City. Most of the characters on our Characters page come from here.

Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty 2023 · CD PROJEKT RED

A spy-thriller expansion set in Dogtown, a new district of Night City. Idris Elba plays the agent Solomon Reed.

Connection to our club: A favourite topic in our fan discussions because of its story and choices.

Cyberpunk 2 In development · CD PROJEKT RED

The sequel to Cyberpunk 2077, first known as Project Orion. It entered pre-production in 2025 and has no release date yet.

Connection to our club: We share every official update about it in the Recent Updates section on our Home page.

Deus Ex: Human Revolution 2011 · Eidos-Montréal

An action role-playing game about Adam Jensen, a security officer with mechanical augmentations, in a world divided over human upgrades.

Connection to our club: Great for fans who like cyberware and stories about what it means to be human.

Ghostrunner 2020 · One More Level

A fast first-person action game where a cyber-ninja climbs a huge tower city.

Connection to our club: For members who enjoy fast action in a neon setting.

Stray 2022 · BlueTwelve Studio

An adventure game where you play as a cat lost in a walled city full of robots.

Connection to our club: A calm and friendly way into the cyberpunk genre for new fans.

Anime and Film

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2022 · Studio Trigger, Netflix

A 10-episode anime about David Martinez, a street kid who becomes an edgerunner, a mercenary outside the law, in Night City.

Connection to our club: The reason many fans joined the club. The title of our Join Us page, "Be an Edgerunner", comes from this series.

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 2026 · Netflix

A new 10-episode story set in Night City, with new characters.

Connection to our club: Our newest discussion topic. See the Recent Updates section on our Home page.

Akira 1988 · Katsuhiro Otomo

An animated film about biker gangs and secret government experiments in Neo-Tokyo.

Connection to our club: A classic that shaped the look of cyberpunk anime, including Edgerunners.

Ghost in the Shell 1995 · Mamoru Oshii, Production I.G

An animated film about a cyborg agent hunting a mysterious hacker.

Connection to our club: It asks the same questions about bodies and minds that Cyberpunk 2077 does.

Blade Runner 1982 · Ridley Scott

A film about a detective hunting artificial humans in a rainy future Los Angeles.

Connection to our club: Its neon streets inspired the look of Night City.

Tabletop Games

Cyberpunk 2013 1988 · R. Talsorian Games

The first Cyberpunk tabletop role-playing game, created by Mike Pondsmith. Players create characters and tell stories together.

Connection to our club: Where Night City was born. Everything on this website started here.

Cyberpunk 2020 1990 · R. Talsorian Games

The second edition of the game. Its timeline includes the Fourth Corporate War, which shapes the world of Cyberpunk 2077.

Connection to our club: Many stories from Cyberpunk 2077 continue events from this edition.

Cyberpunk RED 2020 · R. Talsorian Games

The current edition, set in 2045, between Cyberpunk 2020 and Cyberpunk 2077.

Connection to our club: A great choice for club game nights and for new players.

Shadowrun 1989 · FASA

A tabletop role-playing game that mixes cyberpunk with magic, elves and dragons.

Connection to our club: For fans who want something different from Night City.
